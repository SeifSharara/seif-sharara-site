SMS Consent & Phone IVR Opt-In

How SMS consent is collected and how messaging works

Seif Sharara uses a phone-based IVR process to collect consent before sending informational SMS messages. A caller must affirmatively press 1 after hearing the SMS disclosure before a text message is sent. If the caller does not press 1, no SMS is sent.

Current SMS Opt-In Method

The only SMS opt-in method used for this messaging campaign is verbal consent collected through the business phone IVR. This campaign does not use website forms, QR codes, paper forms, purchased lists, rented lists, affiliate lists, or other SMS opt-in methods.

How the Phone Opt-In Works

  1. A consumer calls the Seif Sharara business phone number.
  2. The call is first routed to the business.
  3. If the call cannot be answered, the caller hears an SMS consent prompt.
  4. The caller is offered the option to receive a text message related to the inquiry.
  5. The caller must press 1 to provide affirmative consent.
  6. If the caller presses 1, consent is recorded and an informational SMS may be sent.
  7. If the caller does not press 1, no SMS is sent and the caller may remain on the line to leave a voicemail.

Current IVR Consent Disclosure

Current IVR Consent Disclosure "Sorry we missed your call. If you'd like Seif Sharara to text you about this inquiry, press 1. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. Otherwise, stay on the line to leave a voicemail."

This disclosure is presented to callers before SMS consent is collected.

Consent Action

The caller provides affirmative consent by pressing 1 on the phone keypad. Pressing 1 is required before an SMS is sent through this IVR opt-in flow.

No Consent = No SMS

If the caller does not press 1, no SMS message is sent through this phone-based opt-in process.

Types of Messages

Individuals who opt in through this phone-based process may receive informational messages related to:

  • Responses to inquiries
  • Missed-call follow-up
  • Appointment scheduling and confirmations
  • Customer support

This phone-based opt-in does not provide consent for promotional or marketing SMS messages. Marketing messages, if ever offered, require separate explicit consent.

Message Frequency

Text message frequency varies depending on the individual's interaction and inquiry.

Rates

Message and data rates may apply.

Opting Out

Recipients may reply STOP at any time to stop receiving SMS messages.

Help

Recipients may reply HELP for assistance.

Consent Is Optional

Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing goods or services.
