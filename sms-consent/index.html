SMS Consent & Phone Opt-In

How SMS consent is collected and how messaging works

Seif Sharara may use SMS to communicate with individuals who have explicitly agreed to receive text messages. SMS consent is optional and is not a condition of purchasing any product or service.

Phone / IVR Opt-In Process

SMS consent may be collected through the following phone-based process:

  1. A person calls the business phone number.
  2. If the call cannot be answered, the caller may be offered the option to receive a text message related to their inquiry.
  3. The caller hears an SMS consent disclosure.
  4. The caller must affirmatively press 1 to agree.
  5. No SMS is sent through this phone-based opt-in flow unless the caller affirmatively agrees.
Proposed IVR Disclosure "Sorry we weren't able to answer your call. If you would like Seif Sharara to send you a text regarding your inquiry, press 1. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out."

Types of Messages

Individuals who opt in through this process may receive:

  • Responses to service or business inquiries
  • Missed-call follow-up
  • Appointment scheduling or confirmation messages
  • Customer support messages

This phone-based consent does not include promotional or marketing messages. Marketing messages, if offered in the future, require separate, explicit consent.

Message Frequency

Text message frequency varies depending on the individual's interaction and inquiry.

Rates

Message and data rates may apply.

Opting Out

Recipients may reply STOP at any time to stop receiving SMS messages.

Help

Recipients may reply HELP for assistance.

Consent Is Optional

Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing goods or services.
